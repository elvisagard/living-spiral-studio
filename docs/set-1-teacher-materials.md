# Set 1 Visual Replication: Teacher Materials

This document contains the teacher-only rubrics and rapid-check keys for Assessments 1A through 1D. Do not attach this document to student assignments.

## Shared Scoring Guidance

Each assessment uses four criteria totaling 12 points. Full credit means the criterion is demonstrated with only immaterial manual-placement variation. Partial credit means the evidence is recognizable but one or more material errors remain. Zero means the evidence is absent, substantially incorrect, or cannot be judged.

Accept minor position, size, spacing, and crop differences when the intended relationship remains clear. Count wrong fonts, materially wrong colors, missing elements, wrong shapes, absent effects, distorted assets, incorrect layering, obvious misalignment, inconsistent repeated elements, or reversed hierarchy as errors.

## Assessment 1A: Decal Replication

### Google Classroom Rubric — 12 points

| Criterion | Full credit | Partial credit | No credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Elements & Content — 2 pts** | **2:** Both exact text groups, large gold circle, heart, and four accent lines are present; nothing extra is added. | **1:** One element is missing, incorrect, or extra, but the product remains identifiable. | **0:** Multiple required elements are absent or the product is not assessable. |
| **Formatting Control — 3 pts** | **3:** Blue-gold-white palette and two fonts match; BE KIND has a strong dark outline; the heart is not distorted. | **1.5:** Core formatting is recognizable, but one or two material errors occur in font, color, outline, or proportional resizing. | **0:** Formatting techniques are mostly absent or substantially incorrect. |
| **Arrangement Control — 3 pts** | **3:** Circle scale, rotated display text, supporting message, heart, accent lines, and layer order match the target. | **1.5:** General arrangement is present, but one or two relationships are obviously misaligned, wrongly sized, or incorrectly layered. | **0:** Placement and relationships do not reproduce the target structure. |
| **Integrated Fidelity — 4 pts** | **4:** The decal immediately reads as a faithful reproduction with the same hierarchy and visual balance. | **2:** It clearly attempts the target and preserves its basic hierarchy, but several visible discrepancies weaken fidelity. | **0:** It is incomplete, redesigned, or does not preserve the target's overall structure. |

### Rapid-Check Key

Check in this order: exact copy; large centered gold circle; outlined and slightly rotated BE KIND; two-line supporting message; undistorted heart; four accent lines; hierarchy and balance. Minor nudging differences do not reduce a score.

Common errors: using a normal text box instead of WordArt, omitting the outline, building the curved region from several shapes, distorting the icon, keeping the gold shape fully on-canvas, or adding decoration.

## Assessment 1B: ID Badge Replication

### Google Classroom Rubric — 12 points

| Criterion | Full credit | Partial credit | No credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Elements & Content — 2 pts** | **2:** All exact text, dog portrait, curved header, slot, Pets icon, divider, and values are present with no unintended additions. | **1:** One required element or text item is missing/incorrect, but the badge remains identifiable. | **0:** Multiple major elements are absent or the work is not assessable. |
| **Formatting Control — 3 pts** | **3:** Portrait is circularly masked and well cropped; border, palette, fonts, fills, and icon treatment match; no asset is distorted. | **1.5:** Image treatment and formatting are recognizable, but one or two material errors occur in masking, crop, border, fonts, color, or proportion. | **0:** Required image treatment or most formatting control is absent. |
| **Arrangement Control — 3 pts** | **3:** Header, portrait layers, text block, icon, footer, margins, and spacing reproduce the target relationships. | **1.5:** Overall arrangement is recognizable, but one or two major alignment, spacing, sizing, or layering errors remain. | **0:** The target's arrangement and layer relationships are not reproduced. |
| **Integrated Fidelity — 4 pts** | **4:** The badge immediately reads as a faithful reproduction with clear hierarchy and balanced spacing. | **2:** It preserves the general badge structure and hierarchy but several discrepancies weaken fidelity. | **0:** It is incomplete, redesigned, or lacks the target's integrated structure. |

### Rapid-Check Key

Check in this order: complete copy; curved green header; true circular mask; usable dog crop; visible white portrait border; slot; consistent centering and margins; undistorted Pets icon; divider and values; clear name-role hierarchy.

Common errors: stretching the dog portrait, using a square crop under a circle, omitting the white portrait border, making the green lower edge straight, inconsistent margins, or using the wrong icon.

## Assessment 1C: Event Ticket Replication

### Google Classroom Rubric — 12 points

| Criterion | Full credit | Partial credit | No credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Elements & Content — 2 pts** | **2:** All exact text, three major regions, dashed divider, three icons, and two stars are present with no extra content. | **1:** One required item is missing or incorrect, but the ticket remains identifiable. | **0:** Multiple major elements are absent or work is not assessable. |
| **Formatting Control — 3 pts** | **3:** Palette and fonts match; panels, divider, and icons are correctly formatted; vertical text is rotated; the stub star is dark blue for contrast. | **1.5:** Core formatting is recognizable, but one or two material errors occur in color, font, rotation, stroke, sizing, or icon proportion. | **0:** Most required formatting techniques are absent or incorrect. |
| **Arrangement Control — 3 pts** | **3:** Admission strip, main panel, price stub, title, icon row, detail columns, divider lines, and stars are accurately aligned. | **1.5:** General structure is present, but one or two obvious alignment, distribution, spacing, or sizing errors remain. | **0:** Precision and repeated structure do not reproduce the target. |
| **Integrated Fidelity — 4 pts** | **4:** The ticket reads immediately as a faithful, controlled reproduction with clear regions and hierarchy. | **2:** It preserves the overall ticket structure but several discrepancies weaken consistency or fidelity. | **0:** It is incomplete, redesigned, or lacks the target's overall structure. |

### Rapid-Check Key

Check in this order: all copy; left/main/stub proportions; vertical ADMIT ONE; dashed divider; three aligned detail columns; undistorted icons; yellow center star on white; dark-blue stub star on light blue; hierarchy led by **TALENT SHOW**.

Common errors: incorrect dot count, uneven distribution, unrotated code, patterned background, faux perforations, inconsistent icon size, or weak alignment inside the central grid.

## Assessment 1D: Promo Card Replication

### Google Classroom Rubric — 12 points

| Criterion | Full credit | Partial credit | No credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Elements & Content — 2 pts** | **2:** All seven exact text groups, basketball photograph, angled divider, three icons, vertical divider, and footer band are present. | **1:** One required item is missing/incorrect, but the promotional card remains identifiable. | **0:** Multiple major elements are absent or work is not assessable. |
| **Formatting Control — 3 pts** | **3:** Photo is intentionally cropped without distortion; palette/fonts match; icon and shapes are correctly formatted; repeated elements are equal. | **1.5:** Core formatting and image treatment are recognizable, but one or two material errors remain in crop, proportion, font, color, or repeated-element consistency. | **0:** Required image treatment and most formatting control are absent or substantially incorrect. |
| **Arrangement Control — 3 pts** | **3:** Photo panel, angled orange divider, black information panel, title, event details, values, footer, margins, and layers reproduce the target. | **1.5:** Overall composition is recognizable, but one or two major alignment, spacing, sizing, or layer-order errors remain. | **0:** The target's integrated arrangement is not reproduced. |
| **Integrated Fidelity — 4 pts** | **4:** The card immediately reads as a faithful, polished reproduction with the target's hierarchy, balance, and visual energy. | **2:** It preserves the general structure and hierarchy, but several discrepancies weaken fidelity or polish. | **0:** It is incomplete, redesigned, or does not preserve the target's overall structure. |

### Rapid-Check Key

Check in this order: all copy; undistorted basketball crop on the left; angled orange divider; black information panel; BASKETBALL/TRYOUTS hierarchy; three aligned white icons and details; values column; orange footer band; faithful total composition.

Common errors: stretching the basketball photograph, using a straight rather than angled photo edge, weak title hierarchy, inconsistent icon sizing, missing the values divider, misaligning the details, or omitting the orange footer band.
